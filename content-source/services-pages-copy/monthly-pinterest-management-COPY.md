## FAQ

**Is this a contract?**
No — it's month-to-month with no minimum commitment, and you can cancel anytime. Three to six months is the recommended runway to see real traction, since Pinterest is a long-term platform.

**How is this billed?**
Monthly, in advance. Payment is non-refundable once work begins for that month.

**What do I need to provide?**
Products or services, blog posts or landing pages to promote, seasonal priorities, and your brand colors, fonts, logo, and photos.

**Will content follow my existing branding?**
Yes — every pin is created using the brand assets and visual direction you provide.

**How do I know which package is right for me?**
Package fit comes down to volume and involvement: Starter (100 pins) is the entry point, Growth (200 pins) adds priority planning and better reporting, and Pro (300 pins) adds a monthly strategy call and priority support. If your intake shows a different package would fit better, you'll hear about it before work begins.

**Can you guarantee traffic or sales?**
No ethical Pinterest specialist can guarantee specific rankings, impressions, clicks, or sales. This service focuses on strong SEO foundations, consistent publishing, and strategy refinement — results depend on your niche, content, website, and competition, and can take weeks or months to show up.
