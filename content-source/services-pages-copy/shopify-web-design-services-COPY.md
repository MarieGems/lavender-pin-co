**Is this a template website?**
No — every site is designed and built from scratch around your branding, content, colors, and images.

**Can I add more pages to the Starter?**
Yes, for $79 each — or move into the Complete Package if you need a full multi-page store from the start.

**Can I sell products through the Starter?**
Not with the Starter — it's static HTML, not a store. The Complete Package includes full Shopify e-commerce.

**Can I upgrade later?**
Yes. Upgrade to the Complete Package within 6 months of your Starter purchase and $100 of what you paid is credited toward it.

**Where does the Starter site live?**
You get static files you can host on your own domain, GitHub Pages, Netlify, or wherever you prefer — no ongoing platform fee required.

**How many products can the Complete Package handle?**
Up to 20 items are included in the setup — titles, descriptions, images, collections, and basic SEO for each.

**Is the Complete Package refundable?**
No — it's described as a personalized digital service, non-refundable once work begins.
