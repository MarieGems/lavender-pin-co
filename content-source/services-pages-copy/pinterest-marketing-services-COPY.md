## Objection-Handling FAQ

**Do I need a Pinterest business account?**
Yes — it's what gives you access to analytics and Pinterest's business features. Account Setup is available if you don't have one yet.

**Can I purchase without a consultation?**
Yes. No consultation or application required — choose your service, complete checkout, and follow the post-purchase instructions.

**What if I purchase the wrong service?**
If your intake form shows another service would fit better, you'll be contacted before any work begins.

**Will you need access to my accounts?**
Account Setup and Monthly Management may require access to Pinterest, Shopify, your website, scheduling tools, or Canva. Secure collaborator or delegated-access methods are used whenever the platform supports them.

**Is Monthly Management a contract?**
No — it's month-to-month with no minimum commitment, though three to six months is recommended to see real traction. Payment is billed in advance and non-refundable once work begins for that month.

**Can you guarantee more traffic or sales?**
No ethical Pinterest specialist can guarantee specific rankings, impressions, followers, clicks, traffic, or sales — for any of these three services. The focus is building strong SEO foundations, strategic content, and consistent long-term visibility. Pinterest results specifically "may take several weeks or months" of consistent effort.
