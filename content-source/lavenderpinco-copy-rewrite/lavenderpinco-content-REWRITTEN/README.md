# Lavender Pin Co — Full Copy Rewrite

A conversion-copywriting pass over every page and blog post scraped from lavenderpinco.com, done in September 2026. No stats, testimonials, or stories were invented anywhere in this — every fact, quote, and story is either straight from the live site or her own previously published words.

## What's in here

**`/pages/`** — full rewrites of the 8 static pages, each with a headline + 2-3 alternatives, a rewritten subheadline, section-by-section copy, and an annotation explaining the reasoning:
- `home.md` — homepage
- `about-and-contact.md`
- `portfolio.md`
- `pinterest-audit.md` — the $197 offer page (added a missing Problem section — see the annotation)
- `checklist-and-faqs.md` — the free lead magnet page (barely touched, it was already the strongest copy on the site) + the Pinterest Marketing FAQ knowledge base

**`/blog/`** — three files covering all 76 posts:
- `cluster-pinterest-seo-foundations-DEDUPLICATED.md` — the 24 posts that shared one near-identical template (5 of them were word-for-word duplicates of each other). Each now has a genuinely distinct angle, hook, and in most cases a different structure.
- `cluster2-pin-copy-account-DEDUPLICATED.md` — 6 more posts with the same duplicate problem (pin titles/descriptions/profile/board/mistakes/checklist), fixed the same way, plus the 2 already-excellent long-form guides (Pin design, Pinterest + email) with just sharpened headlines.
- `remaining-posts-headline-hook-pass.md` — the other 44 posts, which were already on distinct topics. These got sharpened headlines/hooks per the copywriting style rules, with personal story content deliberately left untouched (see below).

## The two big fixes

**1. Thirty posts were the same article wearing different titles.** Five were literally word-for-word identical. That's now fixed — every post answers a genuinely different question.

**2. The audit page (your $197 offer) had no Problem section.** It jumped straight from the headline to "how it works" without ever naming the reader's actual frustration first. Added one short section for that; everything else on that page was already close to right.

## What I deliberately did NOT touch

The May–July story content — the origin story, the mom-of-7 balance post, the burnout post, the Facebook-post-to-AEO-audit story — is the best-differentiated writing on the site, and it's yours in a way generic copywriting principles shouldn't mess with. I sharpened a couple of opening hooks using only words you already published, and left the rest alone on purpose. Rewriting authentic first-person material to sound more "optimized" usually makes it worse, not better.
