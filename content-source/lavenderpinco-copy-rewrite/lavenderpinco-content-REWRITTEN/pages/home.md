**Subheadline:**
Personalized Shopify web design and Pinterest marketing for small businesses and product brands — built around what your customers are actually searching for, not a template with your logo dropped in.

**Primary CTAs (kept — already specific and benefit-led):**
- Grow My Business With Pinterest →
- Build My Shopify Store →

**Proof line (unchanged, already factual/specific — do not inflate):**
3 Pinterest Verified Merchant stores · SEO-focused Shopify websites · Personalized one-on-one service

*Annotation:* The original headline ("Build a Better Website. Get Found on Pinterest.") tells, it doesn't show a gap. The rewrite names the actual discomfort — good products, invisible on the platform built to surface them — which is the Human Action Model's first beat. Run it through "Now you can…": "Now you can... make sure Pinterest actually knows what you're selling." That's concrete and true, not a stretch.
