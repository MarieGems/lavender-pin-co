# About Page — Rewrite
Original: https://lavenderpinco.com/pages/about

---

**Headline:**
You Don't Need a Pinterest Person AND a Web Person. You Need One Who Gets Both.

**Headline alternatives:**
- A: "The Strategist Who Sees Your Whole Customer Journey" — differentiation-focused
- B: "Why Lavender Pin Co Handles Pinterest and Shopify Together" — direct, answers the "why not hire separately" objection up front

**Subheadline:**
When you work with Lavender Pin Co, you're not passed between departments or handed a generic strategy — you work directly with someone who takes the time to understand your business, audience, and goals.

**Body:**
A background in web design, ecommerce, SEO, and Pinterest marketing means looking at your complete customer journey — from how someone discovers your business on Pinterest to what they experience the moment they land on your site. Most businesses hire one specialist for traffic and a different one for the website that traffic lands on, and the two rarely talk to each other. Here, they don't have to.

## How I Work

**Headline:** Strategy Before Design

- **SEO-first strategy** — Keyword research and search intent guide your website structure, Pinterest content, and optimization from day one.
- **Customized for your business** — No cookie-cutter websites or generic strategies. Every project is shaped around your business and goals.
- **Shopify-focused web design** — A professional, mobile-friendly Shopify website with clear navigation and an organized customer journey.
- **Pinterest search strategy** — A presence focused on keywords, discoverability, strategic content, and long-term traffic.
- **Professional visual design** — A polished, consistent experience across your website and Pinterest content.
- **Personalized support** — Work directly with a Shopify designer and Pinterest specialist who knows your project start to finish.

CTA: Ready to talk about your business? →

*Annotation:* The original About page is nearly a duplicate of the homepage's personalized-support section — same six paragraphs, same six bullet points, word for word. That's fine for the homepage teaser, but the About page itself should be doing different work: answering "why should I trust one person to do both jobs" specifically, since that's the actual hesitation a visitor has by the time they click through to About. Rewrite leans into that instead of repeating the homepage.

# Contact Page — Rewrite
Original: https://lavenderpinco.com/pages/contact

---

**Headline:**
Tell Me Where Your Website or Pinterest Is Stuck

**Headline alternative:**
- A: "Ready to Talk About Your Business?" (original — kept as a safe, warm alternative if the direct version feels too bold for this audience)

**Subheadline:**
Share a little about your business, website, and goals. I'll personally review your message and respond within 1–2 business days — no auto-reply, no sales team.

**Details (unchanged — clear and specific already):**
- Email: hello@lavenderpinco.com
- Response time: Typically within 1–2 business days
- Service area: Remote Shopify web design and Pinterest marketing across the United States
- Personalized support: Every message is personally reviewed

**Form fields (unchanged):** Name, Email Address, Business Name, Website URL, Service Interest (Pinterest SEO Audit / Pinterest Business Account Setup / Monthly Pinterest Management / Shopify Web Design / Shopify Website Redesign / Pinterest and Shopify Services / Not Sure Yet), Message

**CTA button:** Send Message

**Closing line (unchanged — this is the strongest line on the original page, keep it exactly):**
Every message is read personally — no forms into the void.

*Annotation:* Left the mechanics alone — email, response time, and form fields are already clear, specific, and honest, which is exactly what a contact page needs. The only change is the headline, softening the generic "ready to talk" into something that names the actual reason someone's on this page (something's stuck).
