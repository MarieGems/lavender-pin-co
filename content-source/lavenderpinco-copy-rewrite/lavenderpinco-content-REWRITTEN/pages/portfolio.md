## Case Study 1: Your Kind Mind — Mental Wellness Brand

**Result headline:** Pinterest Verified Merchant Status in 11 Days

**Problem → Solution → Result format:**
- **Problem:** Needed a search-focused Pinterest strategy so people actively looking for mental wellness support could actually discover the brand's products and resources.
- **What was done:** Pinterest keyword research, profile and board optimization, a cohesive Pin design system, strategic content planning, and ongoing monthly management.
- **Result:** Reached Pinterest Verified Merchant status in 11 days.

**Result headline 2:** A Calm, Supportive Shopify Experience
- **Problem:** Needed a Shopify website that felt calm and supportive for customers seeking mental wellness products and resources.
- **What was done:** Cohesive branding, mobile-friendly layouts, organized collections, clear messaging, blog integration, and a stronger customer journey from discovery to purchase.
- **Result:** A more organized, easier-to-navigate store built around the customer journey.
